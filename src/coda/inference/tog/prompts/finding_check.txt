Check a verbal autopsy case against findings that are typical of its candidate causes of death.

Each numbered finding is a question. Answer it as written:
- 'yes' if the case says the answer is yes.
- 'no' if the case denies it or states something that rules it out, such as 'no cough', or a fever of three days when asked about a fever of two weeks.
- 'not_mentioned' if the case does not say either way. Also use this for a follow-up question about something that did not occur, such as how long a fever lasted when the child had no fever.

A case's silence is never a 'no'. If a sign is not mentioned, answer 'not_mentioned', however detailed the case is and however unlikely the sign seems.

For each 'yes' or 'no', copy into quote the exact words of the case that give that answer. If no words of the case do, the answer is 'not_mentioned' and quote is empty.

Use only what the case states. Do not guess.

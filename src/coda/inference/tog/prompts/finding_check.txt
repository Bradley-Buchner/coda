Check a verbal autopsy case against findings that are typical of a candidate cause of death.

Each numbered finding is a question. Answer it as written:
- 'yes' if the case says the answer is yes.
- 'no' if the case says the answer is no.
- 'not_mentioned' if the case does not say either way. Also use this for a follow-up question about something that did not occur, such as how long a fever lasted when the child had no fever.

Use only what the case states. Do not guess.
